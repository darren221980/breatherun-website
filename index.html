Your privacy comes first

BreatheRun is designed to help you run, not collect your personal data. We only use the permissions needed to deliver the features you choose.

📍 Location During Runs

GPS is only used while you're recording a run so we can calculate your route, distance and pace.

🔒 Your Data Stays Yours

Your run history is stored on your device. We don't sell your data or build advertising profiles.

🚫 No Unnecessary Tracking

We only request the permissions required for BreatheRun to work. Nothing more.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions before you head out for your next run.

Why does BreatheRun need my location?
Location is used only while recording a run so the app can measure your route, distance and pace.
Do I need to create an account?
No. BreatheRun is designed to work without requiring you to sign up before you can start running.
Does the app work offline?
Most running features work without an internet connection. GPS tracking continues while you're out on your run.
Is BreatheRun suitable for beginners?
Absolutely. BreatheRun has been designed specifically to help new and returning runners build confidence through guided breathing and simple coaching.
