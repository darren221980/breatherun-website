Frequently Asked Questions

Why does BreatheRun need my location?

Location is used only while you're actively recording a run so the app can measure your route, distance and pace.

Does BreatheRun track me all the time?

No. Location is only accessed during an active run recording.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use BreatheRun without creating an account.

Where is my running history stored?

Your run history is currently stored on your device.

Does the app work offline?

Most features work without an internet connection. GPS tracking requires location services but not continuous mobile data.
