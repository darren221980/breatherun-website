Support

Need help using BreatheRun? Hopefully you'll find the answers below.

Getting Started

play_circle

How do I start a run?

Open BreatheRun, tap Start Run and follow the breathing guidance as your session begins.

location_on

Why does the app need location?

Location is used during an active run to calculate your distance, pace and route.

Troubleshooting

volume_up

I can't hear the audio prompts

Check your device volume, Bluetooth connection and notification settings. Make sure media audio is enabled.

satellite_alt

GPS isn't accurate

Give your phone a few moments to obtain a GPS signal before starting your run. For best results, enable High Accuracy location mode.

battery_saver

Battery optimisation

Some Android devices stop background activity to save battery. Consider excluding BreatheRun from battery optimisation if tracking stops unexpectedly.

smartphone

The app isn't working correctly

Ensure you're using the latest version of BreatheRun available from Google Play.
