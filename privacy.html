Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 28 July 2026
Last Updated: 4 August 2026

Your privacy matters to us. BreatheRun has been designed to help you enjoy running without collecting unnecessary personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, why it is needed, and how it is handled.

location_on Information We Collect

Location

If you choose to record a run, BreatheRun accesses your device's location while your run is active in order to calculate your distance, pace and route.

Run History

BreatheRun stores information about completed runs, including distance, duration, pace and breathing session details.

Notifications

If enabled, notifications are used to deliver countdowns, breathing prompts and running guidance.

Health Connect (Heart Rate)

If you have Health Connect installed and a connected device — such as a Garmin watch — that has recorded heart rate, BreatheRun can read the heart rate recorded for the time window of your run and show it alongside your run stats.

If you choose to tap "Sync to Health Connect" on a completed run, BreatheRun writes that run to Health Connect as an exercise session, including distance and steps when available, so compatible apps that read exercise data from Health Connect can access it. This only happens for a specific run when you explicitly choose to sync it; BreatheRun never syncs runs automatically.

Health Connect access is entirely optional and can be granted or revoked at any time from Health Connect's own app settings.

settings How We Use Your Information

Information is used only to provide the core functionality of BreatheRun.

  • Recording your runs.
  • Providing breathing guidance.
  • Displaying your running history.
  • Showing heart rate alongside a run, when a device such as a Garmin watch has recorded it to Health Connect.
  • Writing a run to Health Connect as an exercise session, only when you choose to sync that run.
  • Improving future versions of the app.

shield_lock Data Storage & Security

Your running history is stored locally on your device. If you choose to sync a run to Health Connect, that run's data is also stored by Health Connect and becomes available to other apps you've granted access to it, governed by Health Connect's own privacy and security controls. BreatheRun does not sell your personal information and does not create advertising profiles based on your activity.

cloud Third-Party Services

BreatheRun may rely on trusted Google services such as Google Play Services to provide core Android functionality.

If you choose to use it, BreatheRun also integrates with Android Health Connect to read heart rate and, when you request it, write your run data. BreatheRun can save a completed run to Health Connect so that compatible apps which read exercise data from Health Connect can access it.

Garmin Connect's own Health Connect integration is currently write-only: it can supply data such as heart rate to Health Connect, but it does not read data back from Health Connect. If you use a Garmin device, you can currently:

  • Supply heart-rate data to BreatheRun through Garmin Connect and Health Connect.
  • Export a BreatheRun route and use an appropriate manual import workflow.
  • Use a future direct Garmin integration, if one becomes available.

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

tune Your Choices

  • Grant or revoke location permission at any time.
  • Manage notification permissions through Android Settings.
  • Grant or revoke Health Connect permission at any time through Health Connect's app settings, and choose whether to sync any individual run.
  • Delete the application and its locally stored data.

child_care Children's Privacy

BreatheRun is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

update Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as BreatheRun evolves. The latest version will always be available on this page.
