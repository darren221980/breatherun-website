Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

BreatheRun respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Use

BreatheRun requests permission to access:

  • Location (to record your running route during an active run)
  • Notifications (to provide run guidance and status updates)
  • Activity Recognition (where supported by your device, to improve run tracking)

Location Data

Your location is only accessed while you are actively recording a run. Location is not tracked before or after a run has ended.

Data Storage

Your running history and app settings are stored locally on your device. At this time, BreatheRun does not require you to create an account and does not upload your run data to external servers.

Advertising

BreatheRun does not display third-party advertising and does not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Google Play Services may be used to distribute and update the application. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google Play Services.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as new features are added. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Support page.
